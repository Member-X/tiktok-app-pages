Layers Explained Uploader

A private publishing tool for the Layers Explained channel

Layers Explained makes short, plain-language videos about 3D printing: filaments, print settings, bed surfaces, print failures and how to fix them.

This uploader is a small desktop tool used only by the Layers Explained creator. It takes finished videos and their captions from the creator's own computer and sends them to the Layers Explained TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. Videos arrive as drafts in the TikTok app, where the creator reviews them, adds sound and posts them by hand.

What it does

  • Signs in to one TikTok account (the creator's own) with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads a finished video file and its caption as a draft.
  • Does not post for anyone else, read other people's content, or collect data about viewers.
