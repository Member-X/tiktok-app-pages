Privacy Policy

Layers Explained Uploader · Last updated November 10, 2026

The Layers Explained Uploader ("the Uploader") is a private desktop tool used only by the creator of the Layers Explained channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account. This page explains what information it handles.

Information the Uploader receives from TikTok

  • Sign-in tokens. After the creator approves the app in TikTok, TikTok returns an access token and a refresh token so the Uploader can send videos to that account.
  • Basic account details. The account's display name and open ID, used only to confirm the right account is signed in.
  • Upload status. Whether each upload succeeded, as reported by TikTok.

How it is stored

Tokens and account details are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a private settings file. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's, never shared, and never sold.

What the Uploader does not do

  • It does not collect information about TikTok viewers or other users.
  • It does not read, download or analyse other people's content.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. These web pages don't either.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Deleting the local settings file removes the stored tokens from the computer.

Children

The Uploader is not directed at children and is used only by the channel's adult creator.
